Backstreet Boys: StreamDiffusion and Notch

For a Backstreet Boys tour, Aidan Poole fine-tuned diffusion models and integrated StreamDiffusion with Notch, bringing generative video into a real-time show workflow.

StreamDiffusion is an open-source pipeline for generating images with diffusion models in real time. Notch is a real-time graphics tool used for live shows and broadcast.
