StreamDiffusion, fine-tuning and Notch

Aidan Poole develops real-time diffusion workflows, including model fine-tuning and integrating StreamDiffusion with Notch. The work adapts diffusion models and brings generative video into a real-time production workflow.

StreamDiffusion is an open-source pipeline for generating images with diffusion models in real time. Notch is a real-time graphics tool used for live shows and broadcast.
