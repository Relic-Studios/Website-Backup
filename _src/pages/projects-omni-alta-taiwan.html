{{> num}}LED content

OMNI and ALTA, Taiwan

Aidan Poole made LED content for two nightclubs in Taiwan, OMNI and ALTA, working with the production agency 36North in early 2024.

Each club has its own screen layout, and every loop was built to that club's pixel map: at OMNI the upstage wall and the two stage wings, at ALTA the main wall, the DJ booth and the balcony strips. The loops were made in slow, fast and on-the-beat versions to run with the music. The looks included liquid noise, circuits, glass, smoke and reflective abstracts.
