Rohki and Project Odyssey

Aidan Poole contributed to Rohki as an AI animation and pipeline developer, working across generative video workflows and creative production. Under the credit RelicVisuals, he was part of the team behind RŌHKI – Episode 1 Trailer, which won first prize in the Narrative category of Project Odyssey Season 1.

The award

Project Odyssey's Season 1 winners page lists the trailer as Best Series Trailer and credits ROHKI, RelicVisuals, PixelPoetryX and Mrboofy. The organizer's own upload of the film is titled "RŌHKI - Episode 1 Trailer - 1st Place, Narrative". The award belongs to that team.

The role

On the Rohki animated series Aidan developed systems and pipelines for AI-assisted animation and contributed creative design. His LinkedIn lists the project from March to October 2024.
