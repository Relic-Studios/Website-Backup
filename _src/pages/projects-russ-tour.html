Russ tour visuals

In July 2025 Aidan Poole made 3D content for four song clips on the Russ tour, working with the production company Something Good.

Each clip is built around a horse figure in a different material: molten lava bound in gold chains, cracked metal shards, engraved gold, and a horse made of stacked cash. He modelled, textured and rendered the clips in Cinema 4D, Octane, Blender and Substance Painter.
