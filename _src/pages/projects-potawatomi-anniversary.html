{{> num}}Anniversary film

Potawatomi Hotel & Casino

For the 35th anniversary of Potawatomi Hotel & Casino in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, Aidan Poole made the 3D and visual-effects sections of a five-minute film for the venue's LED screens, working with the production agency 36North.

Relic Studios made four of the film's six scenes: the grasslands, the industrial age, the present day, and a finale in which the casino is built and opens under fireworks. Each scene was rendered to fit the venue's screens: a wide main wall, four tall pillar screens and the sportsbook pillars.
