01Biography

Aidan Poole, known artistically as Relic and RelicVisuals, is a visual artist, creative technologist and founder of Relic Studios based in the San Francisco Bay Area. His work combines cinematic 3D animation, live visual performance and large-scale display content with generative video, model fine-tuning and AI software development.

His work with Rohki includes AI animation workflows, production pipeline development and creative design. Credited as RelicVisuals, he contributed to RŌHKI – Episode 1 Trailer, the first-prize Narrative winner at Project Odyssey Season 1. The organizer credits ROHKI, RelicVisuals, PixelPoetryX and Mrboofy, with the entry listed as Best Series Trailer.

His entertainment work includes video-wall artwork for the Sky-Bar at Real Madrid's Santiago Bernabéu Stadium and motion graphics for 36North's Dae-V / Bad Bunny project in Puerto Rico. His production experience also includes StreamDiffusion work for Backstreet Boys. Across his real-time AI practice, he fine-tunes diffusion models and integrates StreamDiffusion with Notch.

He develops interactive visual systems using TouchDesigner and generative AI, including work presented at the Daydream Real-Time AI Summit and ComfyUI events. His broader visual practice spans 3D sculpting, animation, VJ performance and content for LED displays and immersive environments.

Beyond visual production, Poole creates AI applications and tools through Relic Studios. These include ATLAS Voice, a Windows application for voice agents in group conversations, and Engram, an agent memory system published on the Relic-Studios GitHub account. His work brings creative direction, technical development and production delivery into the same practice.
